How to play

Gems fall toward the hit line. Press the matching key exactly as a gem crosses it.

Left hand
Right hand
  • Click Play to start — your browser must be unlocked by a click so audio can begin.
  • Hit the note as it crosses the line. Timing is everything: Perfect > Great > Good.
  • Build a combo to raise your multiplier. A miss resets it.
  • Press Esc or P to pause at any time.
  • On touch devices, tap the keys along the bottom of the screen.
  • The keys and lanes change with the difficulty: fewer lanes on Easy, all eight on Hard.
